How the lab works

  1. Predict. Choose an answer before the trace runs.
  2. Rate your confidence. A sure but wrong prediction marks an idea to revisit.
  3. Reveal. Follow the execution or decision trace.
  4. Explore. Open the sandbox at any time and change the model; each completed experiment adds one lab skill.
