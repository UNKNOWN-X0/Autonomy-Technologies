Recursion and Dictionaries Lab

Algorithms, Programming, and Data Representation · Module 23131 · Chapter 8

Commit to a prediction, then watch a call trace or dictionary update unfold one step at a time. The experiments model the lecture’s positive-integer multiplication, 0-based Fibonacci sequence, normalized palindrome test, word counts, and nested-list flattening.

How the lab works

  1. Predict. Choose an answer before the trace runs.
  2. Rate your confidence. A sure but wrong prediction marks an idea to revisit.
  3. Reveal. Follow the call descent, returned values, or changing key–value table.
  4. Explore. Open the sandbox at any time and change its input; generated challenges practise new values.
