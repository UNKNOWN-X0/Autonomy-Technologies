Branching and Iteration Lab

Algorithms, Programming, and Data Representation · Module 92313 · Chapter 6

Predict what Python does before it runs. Then watch it work one step at a time: an expression reduced operation by operation, a slice picking its characters, and a program’s running line, variables and output.

How the lab works

  1. Predict. Commit to an answer before the program runs.
  2. Rate your confidence. A sure but wrong answer marks an idea to revisit first.
  3. Reveal. Watch Python carry out each step, and compare the result with your prediction.
  4. Explore. Press Explore freely at any time, or finish the four challenges, to type your own expressions, strings and loops.
