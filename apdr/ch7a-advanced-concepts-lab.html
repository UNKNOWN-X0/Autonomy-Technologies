Predict how names bind, types dispatch, resources end, compiler stages flow, and recursive calls return. Each experiment isolates one rule from the advanced lecture.

How the lab works

  1. Predict. Choose an answer before the trace runs.
  2. Rate your confidence. A sure but wrong prediction marks an idea to revisit.
  3. Reveal. Follow names, runtime rules, resource ownership, compilation, or recursive returns.
  4. Explore. Open the sandbox at any time and change its input; generated challenges practise new values.
