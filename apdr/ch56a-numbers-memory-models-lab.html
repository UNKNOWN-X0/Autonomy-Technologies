How the lab works

  1. Predict. Choose an answer before the stage reveals the result.
  2. Rate your confidence. A sure but wrong answer appears in Your results as a useful review target.
  3. Watch the steps. Follow the integer or floating-point calculation, cache lookup, or multicore state change.
  4. Explore. Complete all four challenges to unlock the experiment controls.
